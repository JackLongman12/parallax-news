# Parallax News privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Parallax News has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. We don't run any server that collects information about you. The same text is in the app under Settings > Privacy policy.

## What stays on your phone

The app keeps these on your phone and never uploads them:
- the places and topics you follow;
- your watchlist;
- your reading history and thumbs up/down;
- the interest model the app learns from what you open.

To delete them, uninstall the app or tap "Reset" in Settings.

## Services the app contacts

The app downloads public data directly from the services below to show news and prices. Like any website, each one sees your IP address and what was requested (for example, a search term or a ticker). Each handles that under its own privacy policy.

- **News:** Google News, Bing News, GDELT, publishers' RSS feeds, and article pages (for preview images).
- **Outlet and place data:** Wikidata and OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
- **Markets:** Yahoo Finance and CNBC.
- **Crawler feed, market snapshot and app updates:** GitHub and our Cloudflare Worker. The Worker stores nothing about you.

Our crawler runs on GitHub's servers, not on your phone. It also uses official news and market APIs (The Guardian, The New York Times, NewsData.io, GNews, NewsAPI, Finnhub). It never sends them anything about you.

## Location

The app uses your location only if you tap "Near me", and only after Android asks for your permission. It requests your approximate location and rounds it to about 1 km. It then sends that to OpenStreetMap to find your town. The location isn't stored anywhere else. Otherwise, the app guesses your home country from your network's region.

## Children

Parallax News is a general news reader. It isn't directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy ship with app updates and appear in this file's history. For questions, [open an issue](https://github.com/JackLongman12/parallax-news/issues).
