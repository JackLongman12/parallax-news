Parallax News shows how the same story is covered across the political spectrum, from world news down to your own town.

<b>See every side</b>
* Every story groups the outlets covering it and shows their left, center and right split.
* Outlet labels for political lean, factuality, ownership and state affiliation.
* Blindspots: stories one side of the spectrum barely covers.
* Divergent takes: stories both sides cover equally, so you can compare the framing.

<b>Global to local</b>
* Follow continents, countries, states, cities and neighbourhoods.
* Finds local papers, radio and TV for each place.

<b>Your feed, on your phone</b>
* A daily briefing that learns what you read, entirely on the device.
* Thumbs up and down, muted topics and hidden outlets.
* Markets tab with indices, a watchlist, trending tickers and top movers.
* Home-screen widget.

<b>Private by design</b>
No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Open source (MIT).

Market data is for information only and is not financial advice.
